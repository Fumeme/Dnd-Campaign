## Overview
Luminol **magical enhancer** product produced and distributed by [[Wiki/VAS|VAS]]

## Controversy & Independent Investigation
- **Rhazmor's Research:** Rhazmor conducted private, independent investigations into Luminol out of deep skepticism regarding its safety, suspecting hidden side effects and corporate negligence.
- **The Target:** This independent whistleblowing and research made Rhazmor a direct threat to the Board of Executives and regional representatives like **Tobias Rutherford**.
- **The Cover-Up:** To silence his findings, VAS orchestrated a poisoned batch event and used **The Blak Hand** to frame and eliminate him under the guise of a criminal dispute.
