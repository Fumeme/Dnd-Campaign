[[Wiki/VAS Trade Company Arc]]
## Summary
[[VAS|V.A.S.]] is a super [[megacorporation]] and a country at the same time. VAS is made out of 2 bodies working in conjunction [[The Bureaucratic Assembly]], which handles the day-to-day civic governance, infrastructure, and public services of the nation's territories, and [[The Board of Executives]], the supreme corporate entity that sits entirely above the governing body, dictating national law, foreign diplomacy, and resource allocation to serve the company's bottom line.

Most Notably VAS is responsible for the globally popular [[magical enhancer]] product branded as [[Wiki/Luminux|Luminux]]. Other things VAS has accomplished is [[Wiki/MagiTek|MagiTek]].
