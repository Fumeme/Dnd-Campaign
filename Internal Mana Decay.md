## Overview
Internal Mana Decay is the official technical term and corporate jargon used internally by VAS to classify severe magical degradation symptoms.

## Associated Symptoms & Indicators
- Physical weakness and chronic dehydration.
- Reduced or severely impaired affinity for magic.
- Systemic breakdown of internal mana pathways due to exposure to volatile substances (such as **[[Red-15-B]] and [[Black-73-C]]**).

## Corporate Context
- Documented in confidential corporate research linked to human testing trials.
- Covered up or euphemized by VAS PR and compliance departments to protect the rollout of flagship enhancement products.
