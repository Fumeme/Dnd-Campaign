Shendo is a [[Magi-Punk]] country.

## Geography & Infrastructure
- [[Miremouth]] A starting district/location tied to the initial lab siege and the distribution of poisoned batches.
- [[South Hampter|South Hampter:]] A major district hosting public VAS expos, regional administration, and high-security corporate impound facilities.
- **Maintenance Express Tunnels:** Heavy-duty underground transit networks designed for industrial access and rovers.

## Distinct Technology & Vehicles
- **Rovers:** Specialized vehicles native to Shendo that are roughly twice the size of a standard car. 
  - Characterized by large, protruding wheels, open interiors, superior efficiency for long-distance travel, and exceptionally high horsepower and torque.
  - Primarily used by tradespeople, making them the equivalent of heavy-duty pickup trucks.
  - Includes rare or discontinued models (such as Rhazmor's compact custom rover).
